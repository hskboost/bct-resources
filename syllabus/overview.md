# BCT Exam Syllabus

## BCT Levels, Question Structure, Duration and Scoring

BCT has two written exams, A and B, and a separate speaking exam, C.

| Level or paper | Question structure | Items | Total minutes | Scoring and reported skills |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [BCT A](https://hskboost.com/en/exams/bct/bct-a/) | Listening (30), reading (30), writing (10) | 70 | 70 | 100 points per section. Total 300. |
| [BCT B](https://hskboost.com/en/exams/bct/bct-b/) | Listening (50), reading (40), writing (2) | 92 | 145 | 100 points per section. Total 300. |
| [BCT C](https://hskboost.com/en/exams/bct/bct-c/) | Five spoken-response parts (4, 4, 3, 3, 1) | 15 | 25 | Part maxima 80, 100, 120, 120 and 80. Total 500. |

CTI reports scores without a universal passing cutoff for BCT A, B or C.

[Official BCT exam specification](https://www.chinesetest.cn/BCT)
