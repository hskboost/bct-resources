# BCT Business Chinese Scenarios and Keywords for A, B and C

This index groups practice scenarios for the Business Chinese Test. BCT A and B are written exams that include listening, reading and writing. BCT C is a separate speaking exam. The scenarios are practice suggestions.

## BCT A · Basic Workplace and Business Situations

Practice recognizing and using short, routine workplace messages.

| Business scenario | 场景 | Communication focus | Core Chinese keywords |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Introductions and reception | 自我介绍与接待 | Exchange names, roles and contact details | 公司、同事、经理、名片、联系方式 |
| Scheduling and directions | 日程与路线 | Identify appointment details and meeting locations | 预约、时间、会议室、地址、楼层 |
| Product and price inquiries | 商品与价格咨询 | Find prices, quantities and basic product details | 产品、价格、数量、尺寸、优惠 |
| Calls and short messages | 电话与简短留言 | Identify a caller, record a message and arrange a callback | 电话、留言、转告、号码、回电 |
| Notices and office forms | 通知与办公表单 | Read a brief notice and identify information for a form | 通知、填写、姓名、部门、请假单 |

## BCT B · Written Business Coordination

Practice finding details, comparing options and writing clear business messages.

| Business scenario | 场景 | Communication focus | Core Chinese keywords |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Quotations and purchasing | 报价与采购 | Compare specifications, prices and delivery terms | 询价、报价、规格、采购、交货期 |
| Contract and payment terms | 合同与付款 | Identify terms, payment milestones and responsibilities | 条款、付款方式、定金、尾款、结算 |
| Orders and delivery exceptions | 订单与交付异常 | Read status updates and identify changes to the delivery plan | 订单、发货、签收、延迟、补发 |
| Customer complaints and returns | 客诉与退换货 | Summarize a complaint and outline possible responses | 投诉、售后、退货、退款、处理方案 |
| Project reports and minutes | 项目报告与会议纪要 | Extract decisions, deadlines and assigned actions | 进度、纪要、预算、负责人、截止日期 |

## BCT C · Spoken Business Communication

Practice giving updates and responding to other people in spoken workplace situations.

| Business scenario | 场景 | Communication focus | Core Chinese keywords |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Welcoming clients | 客户接待 | Introduce attendees and outline the visit | 欢迎、来访、议程、参观、安排 |
| Presenting a product or service | 产品或服务介绍 | Explain features, benefits and intended users | 功能、优势、适用、演示、反馈 |
| Discussing terms | 商务协商 | State a proposal, explain constraints and summarize agreements | 建议、条件、让步、达成一致、确认 |
| Reporting progress and risks | 进度与风险汇报 | Explain a delay, its impact and next steps | 目前、进展、风险、影响、下一步 |
| Handling a service call | 客户服务通话 | Clarify a problem and agree on a follow-up | 核实、问题、解决办法、回访、跟进 |
